- An example mod from the Play AC Online team: from 1 AM to 3 AM the town shop opens again and sells only gyroids, a different few each night, and now and then the Ghost Lantern, a lantern of the mod's own with its own pictures. Its files show a shop with hours and a mod item that looks like nothing else. This repository held Evening Tea Room 1.0.0 before; After Hours takes its place.
